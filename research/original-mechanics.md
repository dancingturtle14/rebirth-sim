# 1in8b.life mechanics study (2 lives played, v0.7.43) — reference for mechanics only; do not copy text/design
Key mechanics to adopt (adapted to historical eras):
- Setup: world choice, draw mode, life length (30/60/90 min to reach local LE), speed 1x-16x, skip-to-next-decision, auto-decide w/ countdown.
- Birth reveal: animated draw showing candidate places with their probability %; then birth registration card: name, narrative, year, sex, place (city/rural), family income quintile, parents' ages+jobs, housing, religion, LE, under-5 death chance, GDP pc; context note (share of births in that place that year); 4 same-second babies.
- Stats 0-100: health, happiness, knowledge (rank among peers), relationships; drift yearly. Money panel: net worth, annual income with local and world rank, yearly breakdown (expenses like tobacco, parent care), possessions, debt.
- Right panel: family, same-second babies status (with death age+cause), life grid (1 cell/year, darker=happier, outline=avg lifespan), survival curve vs world, era/country stat panel with sources.
- Life log newest-first; many lines carry a stat footnote + source.
- Yearly focus from age 6: study / friends / help family / sport / hobby with effect hints. Ages 0-5 none.
- Decisions: major vs small; modal with grey STATS BOX citing source + options with effect hints and gating (money thresholds, knowledge, admission odds "偏低").
  Types: temperament, pet, schooling continuation (% completing), first job (informal %, farm %, salary per option), university odds, major, romance (avg marriage age), marriage, children (TFR), childcare, vices (smoking %, alcohol, gambling w/ debt chain), health (out-of-pocket cost vs savings), purchases (vehicle + accident rate), housing (price/income), investing, business expansion odds, affair, divorce, relatives, elder care, moral events (wallet, stranger collapse, refugees), retirement/pension coverage, disasters.
- Random events (log only): vaccines, diseases w/ child mortality stats, harvest failures, no electricity, punishment, grandparents/siblings births/deaths w/ cause, festivals, survival milestones ("~95% of boys born same year still alive"), world events (crises w/ base rate, crashes), follow-on chains (pet ages and dies).
- Death: "final year, age N" screen, pixel replay of moments, death record: narrative, cause, percentile vs cohort, years above/below LE, share of deaths same cause at that age, education/job/spouse/children/who saw off/happiest year/places/estate, reflections, 5 same-second lives sorted by lifespan, grid with icons, survival curve, income-rank & net-worth charts by age, memorable moments, full log, share card w/ seed link.
- Probabilities: per place/year/sex tables; income from quintile + Gini; gating by money/stats; poor/rural get extra hardship events. Theme: "choices matter but hard to escape birth statistics".
